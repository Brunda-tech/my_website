🌾 About Us

Who We Are

We are a team of students who developed this digital platform to help local farmers access technology easily.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide farmers with crop information, weather updates, market prices, and government schemes in one single platform.

Why This Platform?

Many farmers lack access to real-time information. This platform bridges the gap between farmers and technology.

Future Scope

In future, we plan to add live weather data, language support, and expert chat features.
